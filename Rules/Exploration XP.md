Reward the party for **exploring the world**, not just for fighting. Each hex the party reaches and investigates grants a flat XP award based on its biome tier. This is calibrated so that thorough exploration supplies **roughly half** of the XP a party needs to level — combat and quests carry the rest.

## How to award

- **Per character.** Each player character gains the listed XP (this matches 5e's per-character XP-to-level table, so it doesn't change with party size).
- **On meaningful discovery.** Award when the party actually *reaches and investigates* a hex — explores the site, finds its secret, or uncovers its lore. Do **not** award for merely passing through a hex on the way somewhere else.
- **Once per hex.** A hex pays out the first time it's properly explored, not on every visit.

## Tier → Biome → Level

| Tier | Biomes | Target party level |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| **Tier 1 — Early** | Forest, Grassland | Levels 1–3 |
| **Tier 2 — Mid** | Oasis, Swamp, Bushland | Levels 4–6 |
| **Tier 3 — Late** | Jungle, Tundra | Levels 7–9 |

*Tundra is the endgame region — a Northrend-style frozen waste riddled with undead, with **Castle Dice (I2)** at its heart. It incorporates the old mountain hexes (mountains and tundra are one biome).*

## The chart

| Tier (target level)       | XP per hex discovered | Notable site (×2) |
| ------------------------- | --------------------- | ----------------- |
| **Tier 1 — Early (L1–3)** | **50**                | **100**           |
| **Tier 2 — Mid (L4–6)**   | **250**               | **500**           |
| **Tier 3 — Late (L7–9)**  | **500**               | **1,000**         |

**Notable site (×2):** any hex flagged in [[Locations Key]] as a **Dungeon / Major location** or a **Hidden** location, or any named landmark or mystery (e.g. piecing together Blackfire Keep's "Dragon's Scar"). Plain **Wilderness / Travel hexes** use the base value.

## Pacing note

5e XP needed to gain each level (per character):

| Level up | XP    | Level up | XP     |
| -------- | ----- | -------- | ------ |
| 1 → 2    | 300   | 5 → 6    | 7,500  |
| 2 → 3    | 600   | 6 → 7    | 9,000  |
| 3 → 4    | 1,800 | 7 → 8    | 11,000 |
| 4 → 5    | 3,800 | 8 → 9    | 14,000 |

The XP curve balloons at higher levels, so per-hex value scales up by tier or exploration would stop mattering late. Each tier's value is set so that discovering most of its hexes yields ≈ half the XP needed to climb that tier's level band (counting only scoring hexes — see *Containment hexes* below):

- **Early** (L1→L4, gap ≈ 2,700): ~27 scoring hexes (Grassland 19 + Forest 8), 4 of them notable (C2, D2, D4, E5). At 40/hex with notables doubled ≈ 1,250.
- **Mid** (L4→L7, gap ≈ 20,300): ~34 scoring hexes (Oasis 6 + Swamp 11 + Bushland 17). At 250/hex ≈ 10,000.
- **Late** (L7→L9, gap ≈ 25,000): ~20 scoring hexes (Jungle 5 + Tundra 14 + D1). At 500/hex ≈ 13,000.

A party that explores thoroughly will reach **level 8–9 by the endgame biomes** off exploration plus the combat and quests already baked into each location.

## Containment hexes (no XP)

The **top row (row 1)** and the **bottom row (A10–C10, plus the open-sea hexes)** exist mainly to keep the party in bounds, not to be explored. They grant **no exploration XP** and are excluded from the pacing math above.

- **Excluded:** A1, B1, C1 (Forest); E1, F1, G1, H1, I1, J1 (Tundra); A10, B10, C10 (Oasis coast) and all open sea.
- **Exception:** **D1** is a deliberate high-level encounter (a dragon's lair). Although it sits in the Forest, it scores at the **Late-tier notable value (1,000 XP)** and counts toward the Late tier — rewarding the party for the challenge, not the map position.
