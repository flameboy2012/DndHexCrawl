Swamp region (E7–G9). Mid tier, party level 4–6. Encounters marked **(Rod)** change if the party carries the [[Rod of Beha]]. Use the swamp's −1 terrain modifier from [[Rules/Random Encounters|Random Encounters]], except on the Serpent Road (road, +1).

## Swamp Travel
- **Pace.** Swamp is ×0.5 (2 hexes a day on foot). See [[Travel]].
- **Swamp fever.** Drinking swamp water, leech bites or a dunking in the black water risk it: **DC 12 Constitution save** (DC 15 at [[F7 – The Wyrm's Ribcage|the heart-pool]]). Symptoms start 1d4 days later: one level of exhaustion that can't be removed by rest. Each dawn, repeat the save; three successes cure it, three failures add another level and start the count again. _Lesser restoration_ or a long rest in [[D8 – The Lantern Grove]] cures it.
- **Sinking mud.** A character who blunders into it is restrained and sinks. Each round, **DC 12 Strength save** to stay put (a failure sinks them deeper). Pulling free: **DC 13 Athletics**, made by the character or an ally within reach. A character who sinks 3 times goes under and starts to suffocate.
- **Drinking water.** All surface water is tainted. Only rainwater, the river above [[E7 – The Rotting Quay]], and the grove pool at D8 are safe.

## The Serpent Road
Beha's old processional road: a raised stone causeway, its gaps patched long ago with boardwalk by traders and trappers, now overgrown and sinking. It runs from the Oasis dunes ([[C8 – The Dune Wastes]]) through [[D8 – The Lantern Grove|D8]], [[E8 – Temple of a Thousand Legs|E8]] and [[F8 - Tomb of Beha|F8]] (the palace hill), then out through [[G7 – The Gate of Heads]] into the Bushland.
- **Pace.** ×1 on the road (4 hexes a day), instead of swamp pace.
- **Collapses.** Each day on the road, roll a d6. On a 1, a section has collapsed: wade round it (lose 2 hours and risk swamp fever), or cross the rotten boardwalk (**DC 12 Dexterity save** each, or fall into sinking mud).
- **Who uses it.** The Haddari, now and then, and the centipede cult, who hunt travellers on it. The map-sellers of [[C5 – Naywich]] send treasure-hunters down it.

### Extra Encounters

Taken Travellers: The party comes upon 3 [cultists](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#cultist_mm) of the centipede cult dragging two bound Haddari along the causeway towards [[E8 – Temple of a Thousand Legs]].
	Hook: if one cultist dies, he carries a jewelled centipede ring (25 gp) and a map to the shrine. The rescued Haddari can tell the party the cult took more of their caravan.

Nellye's Druid: A wood elf [druid](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#druid_mm) named **Ilvarre** is kneeling by a poisoned stream, trying to purify it. It isn't working. She serves [[Queen Nellye]], and she's friendly unless the party carries the rod openly.
	Hook: she sends the party to Brannagh in [[D8 – The Lantern Grove]]. **(Rod)** She draws her scimitar and orders the bearer to leave the swamp.

Tainted Beast: A [giant boar](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#giant%20boar_mm), its hide sloughing away and its tusks black, charges out of the trees. It drank from the black water and went mad.
	Twist: Its acid-burned body hints at what lies upstream: it came from the direction of [[F7 – The Wyrm's Ribcage]].

Rival Treasure-Hunters: Four sellswords (a [veteran](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#veteran_mm) and 3 [thugs](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#thug_mm)) with a map bought in Naywich, looking for "the staff in the swamp king's tomb".
	Twist: Their map was sold by the centipede cult. Following it leads them, and anyone with them, past the shrine at E8. Warning them may save their lives.
