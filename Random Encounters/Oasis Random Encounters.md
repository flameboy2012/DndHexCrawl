Oasis region (A8–C9). Mid tier, party level 4–6. Encounters marked **(Rod)** change if the party carries the [[Rod of Beha]].

| Roll (d12) | Encounter |
| ---------- | --------- |
| 1 | **Sandstorm:** A wall of sand rolls in. Each creature without cover makes a DC 13 Constitution save or gains a level of exhaustion. Visibility drops to 10 ft for 1d4 hours and the party loses its way (DC 15 Wisdom (Survival) to stay on course). |
| 2 | **Giant Scorpions:** 2 [giant scorpions](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#giant%20scorpion_mm) burst from the sand at the party's feet. Their nest nearby holds the bones of a lost traveller and 2d10 gp. |
| 3 | **Pirate Patrol:** A [yuan-ti malison](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#yuan-ti%20malison%20(type%201)_mm) leads 3 [bandits](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#bandit_mm) on a search for "the staff." They demand to know the party's business. **(Rod)** If they see the rod, they attack to take it, or bow if the fragment speaks through the bearer in old yuan-ti. |
| 4 | **Haddari Water Caravan:** 4 nomads and 6 camels from [[B8 – The Dry Well]], hauling water from a distant seep. They trade, share rumours, and warn the party off the pirate camp. |
| 5 | **Mirage:** A lush green oasis shimmers on the horizon. Following it wastes half a day and costs extra water. DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) or Intelligence (Nature) to see through it. |
| 6 | **Dust Devils:** A whirling column of sand carries 4 [dust mephits](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#dust%20mephit_mm) who love to blind and rob travellers. They flee if two are destroyed. |
| 7 | **Wind-Scoured Ruin:** The wind uncovers a fallen pillar or statue carved with the serpent-tail sigil. A quick search finds 1d4 ancient ouroboros coins (25 gp each). **(Rod)** The fragment names the building it once belonged to. |
| 8 | **Ksarra the Brass Dragon:** A [young brass dragon](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#young%20brass%20dragon_mm) lands in front of the party and demands conversation, gossip and news. She serves Baram and knows the Haddari. She fights only if insulted or attacked. **(Rod)** She smells Beha on the party and flies off at once — probably to tell someone. |
| 9 | **Pirate Deserter:** A sunburnt human deserter from [[Captain Szolsassih]]'s crew is dying of thirst under a rock. If given water, he shares the camp's layout and that the captain "lost good crew in that pyramid and now she's paying strangers to die instead." |
| 10 | **Serpent Swarm:** 2 [swarms of poisonous snakes](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#swarm%20of%20poisonous%20snakes_mm) emerge at dusk. **(Rod)** The snakes are drawn to the rod and circle the bearer without attacking. The bearer can command them for one hour. |
| 11 | **The Watching Eye:** A DC 16 Wisdom (Perception) check or _detect magic_ spots an invisible scrying sensor hovering above the party. [[Lady Dice]] is looking for whoever emptied the pyramid. If not noticed, she learns what the party carries. |
| 12 | **Hallowstride's Hunters (night only):** 2 [vampire spawn](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#vampire%20spawn_mm) sent by Lady Hallowstride to find the "staff." By day, the party instead finds a wagon with 2 human thralls ([thugs](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#thug_mm)) guarding two sealed coffins. |

### Extra Encounters

Bulette Hunting Ground: A [bulette](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#bulette_mm) bursts from a dune and starts hunting the party by tremor. Standing still gives advantage on Stealth against it.
	Twist: It has been driven out of the [[C8 – The Dune Wastes|dune wastes]] by something larger beneath the sand.

Pirate Raid on the Haddari: 6 [bandits](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#bandit_mm) and a [yuan-ti pureblood](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#yuan-ti%20pureblood_mm) are robbing a Haddari caravan of its water.
	Complication: Hadra's grandson Teo is among the captives. Saving him earns the Haddari's trust; letting the pirates go earns the pirates' goodwill.

Lamia's Pleasure Garden: A [lamia](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#lamia_mm) hides in a ruined bathhouse, using illusions to make it look like a lush oasis palace. She charms travellers and keeps them as servants.
	Twist: She worshipped Beha as a child of his court's legends. **(Rod)** She offers the bearer anything to touch the rod.

Sandstorm Shelter: During a severe sandstorm the party shelters in a half-buried yuan-ti shrine — along with a group of pirates doing the same. No one can leave until the storm passes (1d6 hours).
	Complication: The shrine's wards wake if anyone draws a weapon, animating 2 [stone guardians](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#animated%20armor_mm) (use animated armor stats) that attack everyone.
