_Artifact / Held by the party_

A 2ft rod of black obsidian crystal. It clearly looks magical and cannot pass for a mundane object, though it can be hidden in a bag.

Recovered by the party from [[A9 – The Pyramid of Beha]].

## Mechanics
- Requires attunement.
- Functions as a **Staff of Power** — https://5e.tools/items.html#staff%20of%20power_dmg
  - _Consider unlocking its powers in stages as the fragment's trust in the bearer grows._

## The Fragment
The rod contains a fragment of [[Beha]]'s consciousness.
- It offers **advice** to whoever is attuned to it, and is genuinely helpful.
- Its goal is possession. If the attuned bearer ever holds the [[Tome of Beha]], the fragment attempts to take control of their body.
- The rod and tome are drawn to each other over time.
- The fragment can **unpick the seals** on Beha's corpse in [[F8 - Tomb of Beha]]. Anyone wanting to open the tomb needs the rod.

## The "Staff" Rumour
Rumours have twisted over two thousand years; nearly everyone searching for Beha's artifact believes it is a **staff**. Only Drongo the bush wizard has worked out it is a rod.

_Idea:_ a faction (pirates or Lady Hallowstride) could be carrying a fake staff they believe is the real thing — a red herring.
