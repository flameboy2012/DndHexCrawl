_Artifact / Held by [[Lady Dice]]_

[[Beha]]'s wizard spellbook. Along with the [[Rod of Beha]], it was a conduit for his power.

## Contents
- High-level spells, including _wish_.
- The **ascension ritual** — the means to become a god.

## Current Status
[[Lady Dice]] holds the tome. Her apprentice Callus took it from Beha's grave in the [[F8 - Tomb of Beha|Tomb of Beha]], then tried to raise Beha as undead, but could not break the seals. The party interrupted the ritual and Callus escaped by dimension door.

Lady Dice wants to use the _wish_ spell to reverse her lichification. She knows a wish is a monkey's paw, so she seeks Beha's knowledge to make it work without backfiring.

## Danger
If the bearer attuned to the rod obtains the tome, the fragment of Beha inside the rod attempts to possess them. The rod and tome are drawn to each other over time.

## Who Wants It
See [[Factions]]. [[Lady Dice]] (has it), [[Queen Nellye]] (wants it gone), and Beha himself.
