_Ruin / Biome Border / Causeway_

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The causeway climbs out of the swamp onto firmer ground, where the trees are thinner and drier. Ahead, two massive stone towers stand on either side of the road, joined by an arch. The gate that hung in it is long gone. Twin minaret-like spires rise from the towers, one of them snapped off halfway up.
>
> Iron spikes line the top of the arch, and something hangs from each one. As you get closer, you can see they are heads: frog-like and bloated, some fresh, some just skulls. Beyond the gate, the road runs north, out of the swamp and into dry, open bushland.

## DM Notes
The **north gate** of Qahessra. In Beha's day, the heads of traitors and failed servants were hung here as a warning. (Inspired by Cairo's Bab Zuweila.) The tradition survived the city: the lizardfolk of [[G8 – The Scaleback Warren]] hang the heads of bullywugs here to mark the edge of their hunting grounds. The heads are fresh, so the lizardfolk visit often.

This is where the **Serpent Road** (the causeway) leaves the swamp for the Bushland. See [[Swamp Random Encounters#The Serpent Road]].

**The inscription.** Above the arch, words in old yuan-ti: _"Bow, for you pass beneath the eyes of the god-king."_ **DC 14 History** or _comprehend languages_ to read it. Under it is a defaced relief: a crowned serpent, its face chiselled away.

## Encounter: The Gatehouse Nest
The broken tower is home to 8 [stirges](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#stirge_mm). They pour out at anyone who climbs the towers, or at dusk.

The intact tower holds a lizardfolk lookout: 2 [lizardfolk](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#lizardfolk_mm) who watch the road. If they see the party coming from the swamp side, one runs to warn the warren while the other hides. A **DC 14 Perception** check spots them. Lizardfolk respect strength. If the party bests the lookout without killing it, they can learn the way to the warren.

## Loot
- In the stirge nest: the dried-out remains of a Haddari trader, with a pouch of 22 gp and a brass compass.
- A lizardfolk cache at the base of the intact tower: 3 javelins and a bundle of dried eel.
