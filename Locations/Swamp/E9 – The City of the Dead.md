_Ruin / Minor Dungeon / Necropolis_

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The ground rises a little here, out of the water, and on it stands a town that no one lives in. Streets of small stone houses with flat roofs and narrow doors, packed tight together, run off in every direction. They're too small for houses: they're tombs. Hundreds of them.
>
> Many of the doors have been smashed in. Bones lie scattered in the street, and in the mud outside one tomb you can see fresh boot-prints, a dropped candle stub, and a circle of salt, half washed away.

## DM Notes
The **necropolis** of Qahessra, on a low rise above the floods, where the city buried its dead in family tombs. (Inspired by Cairo's City of the Dead.) The rich are buried in the middle, in a few grand tombs with domes.

**Callus was here.** [[Lady Dice]]'s apprentice, Callus, raided the necropolis before moving on to [[F8 - Tomb of Beha]]. He was looking for Beha's resting place, breaking into tombs and raising their dead to dig for him. This is where the rumours of "necromancers in the swamp" came from.
- **Clues:** the salt circle, candle stubs, and chalk marks on the doors of tombs already searched: a die-face with one pip. **DC 14 Arcana:** the circle was for _animate dead_. **DC 15 Investigation**: one chalk door is marked with a different sign, an arrow pointing east toward F8.
- If the party has already met Callus, they recognise his work.

## Encounter: The Hungry Dead
Callus left behind what he didn't need, and the opened tombs drew scavengers. A [ghast](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#ghast_mm) leads 4 [ghouls](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#ghoul_mm) through the streets at night, gnawing old bones. By day they sleep in the smashed tombs and come out if the party searches noisily.

## Minor Dungeon: The Vizier's Tomb
In the middle of the necropolis stands the only domed tomb Callus didn't open: the tomb of **Ankhessa**, Beha's grand vizier. The bronze door is sealed with a cobra-shaped lock. (Callus found the right place elsewhere and moved on.)
- **Opening it:** **DC 18 thieves' tools**, or the cobra signet from [[D9 – The Drowned Bazaar]] (_unconfirmed link_).
- **Inside:** three small rooms.
  1. **Antechamber:** painted walls show Qahessra in its glory: the river quay, the bazaar, the palace on its hill. **DC 13 History:** the city's name, _Qahessra_, is written above the palace.
  2. **Offering room:** clay jars of dust (once food and wine), a gilded chair, and a dart trap in the doorway (**DC 14 Perception** to spot; 2d10 poison damage, **DC 13 Dex save** for half).
  3. **Burial chamber:** Ankhessa's sarcophagus. She rises as a [mummy](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#mummy_mm) when the lid moves. Her 2 servants, buried at her feet, rise with her as [zombies](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#zombie_mm).
- **(Rod)** Ankhessa recognises the rod and kneels to its bearer. She speaks old yuan-ti and offers her service to the "returned king". The fragment uses her: through the bearer, it asks her where the tome was taken. She doesn't know. _Idea (unconfirmed):_ she follows the party as an undead servant if the bearer commands it, which would make the fragment very pleased.

## Loot
In the burial chamber: a gold death-mask (250 gp), a lapis scarab necklace (100 gp), and Ankhessa's scroll case with a _spell scroll_ of _speak with dead_ and a crumbling letter in old yuan-ti from Beha to his vizier. _Idea (unconfirmed):_ it orders preparations for the march on the forest and speaks of "the forest queen's heart", a hint at how Beha meant to take [[Queen Nellye]]'s power.
