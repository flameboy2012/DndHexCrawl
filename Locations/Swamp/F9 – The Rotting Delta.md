> On one of the mudbanks lies a boat: a long, narrow rowing boat, tipped on its side, its hull stove in. There's a painted symbol on the bow — an anchor with two serpents wrapped around it.

## DM Notes
Where the river reaches the sea. The channels shift with every storm. (Inspired by the Nile delta.) The tide turns every 6 hours; at high tide the sea floods the flats, and the channels are too deep to wade.

## Hazards
- **The tide.** If the party is caught on the flats when the tide turns, they have 1 hour to reach high ground. After that, the water rises past waist height: swimming only (**DC 12 Athletics** each hour, or a level of exhaustion).
- **Leeches.** Any character who wades the channels comes out with leeches. **DC 12 Constitution save** or contract swamp fever (see [[Swamp Random Encounters#Swamp Travel]]).

## The Wreck
A longboat from [[Captain Szolsassih]]'s crew. Six pirates rowed up the delta to search the swamp for "the staff". A crocodile overturned the boat.
- Two bodies are tangled in the wreck. The others never came back.
- In a waxed bag under the thwart: a scrawled map of the swamp marking the necropolis ([[E9 – The City of the Dead]]) and the palace hill ([[F8 - Tomb of Beha]]), with the note _"the tomb — staff must be with the body."_ The pirates are behind the times: the tome is gone and the party has the rod.
- A small chest: 45 gp, a spyglass (cracked lens, 300 gp if repaired, 50 gp as is), and a bottle of rum.

## Encounter: The Old Mother
A [giant crocodile](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#giant%20crocodile_mm) lives in the deepest channel. She sank the longboat and waits for more. Anyone who swims or wades a deep channel draws her. A **DC 15 Perception** check spots the eyes and nostrils above the water.
