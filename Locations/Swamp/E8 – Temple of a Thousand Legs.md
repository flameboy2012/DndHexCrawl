_Dungeon / Cult Lair_

![[centipede temple.webp]]

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The old causeway rises out of the muck here, its paving stones cracked and furred with moss. Beside it squats a stone shrine, half sunk into the swamp so that only its roof and a short flight of steps still show. The steps lead down into darkness. Someone has hung a garland across the doorway, but it isn't flowers: it's the empty, segmented shells of centipedes, each as long as your arm, strung together and clicking softly in the breeze. The mud around the steps is churned with footprints, and drag marks.

## DM Notes
A shrine of [[Beha]]'s city, sunk into the swamp. Below the steps lies a small temple where Beha's priests once worshipped him as a living god. The city drowned, the priests died, and giant centipedes moved into the flooded vaults.

A few years ago a starving outcast named **Connerath Glynt** stumbled in and was bitten. The poison broke his mind: he came to believe the centipedes were gods who had spared him to serve them. He began dragging victims to the centipedes, and the creatures learned to spare those who feed them. Other outcasts, each poisoned into madness, joined him. They call themselves the **Children of a Thousand Legs**. Lately the kidnappings have picked up.

The cult is a **standalone side dungeon**. It has nothing to do with Beha's artifacts; the cultists only squat in his ruins.

**Source:** _Temple of the Centipede Cult_ by Jack Kaiser, in _Fantastic Lairs_ (pp. 10–14, written for four to five 2nd-level characters). It is condensed below in my own words, reskinned for this campaign and scaled for a **level 5 party of four** (see _Scaling_). Full room text is in the PDF. The map above is keyed with the module's area numbers (T1–T4; S marks the secret doors at each end of T3).
- Original: duergar temple to Asmodeus. Here: a temple to Beha the god-king. The statues are yuan-ti, and the specter is a yuan-ti priest.

## Hooks
- **The missing caravan.** A Haddari water caravan from [[B8 – The Dry Well]] vanished on the causeway. At their last camp the searchers found a centipede's head-shell left in place of the people. Hadra Sunmantle begs the party to find them. One of them is the prisoner in T2.
- **The map-sellers.** In [[C5 – Naywich]] a young hawker sells maps to "the staff in the swamp king's tomb". The maps lead travellers along the causeway, straight past the shrine. The hawker works for the cult, and there are others like them. This plays on the "staff" rumour (see [[Rod of Beha]]).
- **The dead kidnapper.** Swamp encounter: a cultist killed in a botched kidnapping (see [[Swamp Random Encounters]]). On the body are a jewelled centipede ring (25 gp) and a map marking the shrine.

## The Cultists
Humans: deserters from [[Captain Szolsassih]]'s crew, Haddari exiles and lost treasure-hunters, all driven mad by centipede poison. They are calm and smiling, with slow, glassy eyes, and many have bite scars on their forearms. They fight to the death.

## Rooms

### T1. The Narthex
> [!quote] Read Aloud
> At the bottom of the steps, a square hall smells of wet stone and old ash. A statue of a robed snake-man stands in each corner, taller than a man. A rune-carved circle in the floor surrounds a faintly glowing symbol: a serpent swallowing its own tail. Against the east wall stands a stone rack of clay urns, many cracked, ash spilled across the floor beneath them.

- The urns hold the ashes of Beha's priests. The cult has swapped some for centipede shells, pieced together and scratched with crude glyphs. **DC 13 Religion** (or speaking old yuan-ti): the circle and runes are a shrine to Beha as a god.
- **The last priest.** A yuan-ti priest's ghost, **Ssaveth**, rises from the ash and demands: _"Name the god-king."_ Use the [specter](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#specter_mm) stat block, but it speaks Common and old yuan-ti.
  - Answer **"Beha"** and Ssaveth lets the party pass, muttering that the others "lied too". Any other answer: it laughs and vanishes, and the statues attack.
  - **DC 12 Persuasion** (with respect): Ssaveth hates the cultists for worshipping vermin in its god's house. It tells the party about the secret passage (T3) and how to avoid its gas trap.
  - **(Rod)** If the party carries the [[Rod of Beha]], Ssaveth bows low and calls the bearer _"my king"_. It answers any question about the temple. The fragment is pleased and says nothing.
- **Statues.** One [animated armor](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#animated%20armor_mm) per character (AC 16). The fight alerts the cultists in T2.
- **Bracelet of pain.** One urn hides a spiked cold-iron bracelet. Putting it on deals 1d4 piercing and lowers the wearer's hit point maximum by the same until it's removed. The wearer can cast _scorching ray_ once (+7 to hit), and then it shatters.
- **North door** to T4: locked. Kathera (T2) and Glynt (T4) carry keys. **DC 16 thieves' tools**; it can't be forced. **DC 16 Perception** at the door: Glynt preaching his story.

### T2. The Sacristy
> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A big stone table fills the middle of this room, and it is wet with blood. Robed figures are working at it with knives. In the far corner, someone is slumped in an iron cage. The walls are carved with scenes of snake-men kneeling before a crowned serpent.

- **Kathera the Stinger** ([cult fanatic](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#cult%20fanatic_mm)) with her pet [giant centipede](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#giant%20centipede_mm) **Kaliv** wrapped round her waist, 1 **Black Spine** ([berserker](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#berserker_mm)), and 1 [cultist](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#cultist_mm) per two characters. They are butchering a dead prisoner for the centipedes. If alerted, they can't be surprised.
- **The prisoner.** **Nadi**, a Haddari water-carrier ([commoner](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#commoner_mm)), the last of the missing caravan. The other three are already dead. She is drugged and remembers little. **DC 14 Persuasion:** how she was taken, and that she saw a ghostly snake-man walk through the north wall (the secret door). Cage: keys, **DC 13 thieves' tools**, or **DC 16 Athletics**.
- **Secret door** to T3: passive Perception 16 or **DC 16 Perception**. To open it, pull one brick out, turn it, and push it back in. The door rises into the ceiling and drops again after 1 minute.

### T3. The Forgotten Passage
> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Dust lies thick and undisturbed in this narrow passage, and cobwebs hang like curtains. The carvings on the walls show an army of snake-men marching on a forest.

- Unknown to the cult.
- **Gas trap** two-thirds of the way along, a 10-ft section of floor. **DC 16 Perception** spots the groove; **DC 14 Investigation** finds the mechanism. Jump it or disarm it (**DC 16 thieves' tools**). If triggered: 40-ft cloud, **DC 14 Con save** or 14 (4d6) poison damage.
- **Statue door.** The passage dead-ends at a peephole into T4. The far side is a statue on hinges. Squeezing through quietly takes **DC 11 Stealth**, with disadvantage unless the hinges are oiled. Success means the cultists are surprised.

### T4. Temple of a Thousand Legs
> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Four braziers fill this vaulted temple with a sickly green light. A wide pit gapes in the middle of the floor, and north of it stands a stone altar slick with blood and worse. Two cracked pillars rise to the south. Statues of snake-men stand against three walls, but every one of them has had its head knocked off and replaced with the huge, hollow head of a centipede. The tallest statue, to the north, holds four more centipede heads in its hands.

- **Connerath Glynt** ([priest](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#priest_mm)) leads a ceremony over a fresh victim on the altar, with 1 [cult fanatic](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#cult%20fanatic_mm), 1 **Black Spine** ([berserker](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#berserker_mm)) and 2 [cultists](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#cultist_mm). Through the south door they are alerted at once; through the statue door they may be surprised.
- **Glynt's tactics.** He opens with _spirit guardians_, a whirl of ghostly centipedes around him, then _spiritual weapon_ (a spectral centipede bursting from the floor) and _guiding bolt_ in a sickly green light. He stays near the altar. Cornered, he throws down his centipede bracelet (see _Treasure_).
- **Pillars.** Hollow. When the fight starts, 1 [giant centipede](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#giant%20centipede_mm) per two characters crawls out.
- **The Child of a Thousand Legs.** In round 2 Glynt calls to the pit, and a huge centipede climbs out. It acts right after Glynt. Use the [carrion crawler](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#carrion%20crawler_mm) stat block (its paralysing tentacles become a numbing bite).
- **Unholy altar.** Within 5 ft of it, Glynt has advantage on spell attacks, and targets have disadvantage on saves against his spells. To break it: **DC 15 Religion** check (calling down divine wrath), any radiant spell, **DC 15 Athletics**, or damage (AC 14, 35 hp, resistant to poison and psychic). It radiates necromancy.
- **The pit.** 20 ft wide and 20 ft deep, with 2 [swarms of insects](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#swarm%20of%20insects_mm) (centipedes) waiting at the bottom. A fall deals 2d6 bludgeoning. Climbing out: **DC 14 Athletics**.
- **Braziers.** **DC 14 Athletics** tips one: 15-ft cone of coals, **DC 13 Dex save**, 14 (4d6) fire damage, or half on a success.

## Treasure
- **Glynt's bracelet** (uncommon, requires attunement): a golden centipede. Once per dawn, speak its command word and throw it down: it becomes a [giant centipede](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#giant%20centipede_mm) under your control for 1 hour or until it drops to 0 hp.
- The **bracelet of pain** (T1).
- The cult's takings, in a chest behind the north statue: 180 gp, 3 ancient ouroboros coins (25 gp each), and the Haddari caravan's water-skins and trade goods. Returning the goods to Hadra earns her trust.

## Scaling
The module is written for level 2. The numbers above are tuned for **four level 5 characters**. The T4 fight is _hard_ (about 3,900 adjusted XP).
- **Level 4:** in T4, drop the Black Spine and use a [giant centipede](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#giant%20centipede_mm) with 30 hp and Large size for the Child, as the module does. Make Kathera a [cultist](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#cultist_mm) with 20 hp.
- **Level 6:** add a second Black Spine to T4, and give the Child maximum hit points.
- **Five characters:** add one cultist to T2 and one giant centipede to T4.
