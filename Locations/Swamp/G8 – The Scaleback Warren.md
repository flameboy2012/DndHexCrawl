## DM Notes
Home of the **Scaleback** lizardfolk: about thirty adults. They're primitive, territorial and practical. Not evil, but they see outsiders as competition or food. There's no trade and no hospitality here. Respect is earned by strength.

They hunt the whole eastern swamp and raid the bullywugs of [[G9 – The Croaking Flats]]. They hang bullywug heads at [[G7 – The Gate of Heads]] to mark their land.

**The Black Mother.** The tribe worships the dragon's skull at [[F7 – The Wyrm's Ribcage]] and believes the rot is her blessing: it keeps soft-skins away. Their shaman paints his scales black with mud from the heart-pool. Anyone who damages the bones, or tries to cleanse the land, becomes their enemy.

## Meeting the Tribe
- **Approaching openly:** 6 [lizardfolk](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#lizardfolk_mm) meet the party at the shore with spears. Gethrak hears them out if the party offers something: meat, a weapon, or a hand against the bullywugs.
- **Challenge:** a character can demand single combat with a champion (a lizardfolk with max hp). Winning earns safe passage through Scaleback land, and the lookouts at G7 let the party pass.
- **A deal:** Gethrak wants the bullywug chief at G9 dead. Do it, and the tribe shares what they know: where the city's dead are buried ([[E9 – The City of the Dead]]), and that "the bone-wizard" (Callus) dug there "two moons ago".
- **Attacking the warren** is a mistake: the whole tribe, plus 2 [giant lizards](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#giant%20lizard_mm), defends it.
- **(Rod)** Ssum smells Beha on the rod and screams that the party carries "the one who killed the Mother". The tribe becomes hostile at once. Hiding the rod deep in a pack beats the smell (**DC 15 Stealth**, or a bag of holding).

## Loot
Tribute in Gethrak's hut: a rusted pirate cutlass, a bronze helmet from Qahessra's guard (30 gp to a collector), 60 gp in mixed coins pulled off drowned travellers, and a string of black dragon scales Ssum wears as a necklace.
