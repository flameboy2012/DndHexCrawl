_Ruin / Biome Border / River_

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The river slides south out of the grassland, clear and quick, and then, over the space of a few hundred yards, it changes. The water darkens to the colour of strong tea, then to oil. A film of rainbow scum gathers in the reeds. The smell hits you: rot, and something sharp underneath it that stings your nose.
>
> Where the water turns, a line of tall standing stones marches across the riverbank, each carved with overlapping scales. Beyond them, half sunk in the mud, lie the remains of a stone quay: mooring rings green with age, and a flight of broad steps going down into the black water, numbered with marks carved up the side.

## DM Notes
This is where the river from [[D1 – Jiaolong's Lair|Jiaolong's]] spring enters the swamp, and where the old city of **Qahessra** began. The quay was the city's river harbour. The numbered steps are a **flood gauge**: priests read the river's height here each year to forecast the harvest. (Inspired by Cairo's Nilometer.)

**The scale stones.** [[Jiaolong]] set them up long after the war to mark where his river ends and the dead dragon's taint begins (see [[F7 – The Wyrm's Ribcage]]). He will not pass them. **DC 14 Arcana** or _detect magic_: faint abjuration; the stones keep the taint from creeping upstream. Breaking one would let the rot spread into the grassland. _Idea (unconfirmed):_ Jiaolong would take that very badly.

**The flood gauge.** The highest mark is labelled in old yuan-ti: _"The year the sky burned."_ It sits 30 ft above today's water. **DC 15 History:** a flood that size would have drowned the whole lower city.

## Encounter: The Well Below
A [water weird](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#water%20weird_mm) lives in the flooded gauge well at the foot of the steps. It was once bound to guard the gauge; the taint has turned it black and spiteful. It attacks anyone who goes down the steps past the waterline. It can't leave the well.

## Hallowstride's Watcher
A single bat hangs under the quay, watching. It belongs to Lady Hallowstride ([[D6 – Hallowstride Manor]] is close by) and flies home at dusk. **DC 15 Perception** spots it. **DC 13 Animal Handling** or _speak with animals_: "the lady wants to know who goes into the bad water". She watches the swamp but rarely acts.

## Loot
At the bottom of the gauge well (once the water weird is dealt with): a silver offering bowl, black with tarnish (40 gp), and 2d6 ancient ouroboros coins (25 gp each) thrown in for luck by long-dead river traders.
