_Minor Site / Fey Grove_

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The little lights lead you on, always just out of reach. They bob between dead trees and over pools of black water, and then, quite suddenly, the swamp stops.
>
> You step into a ring of green. The grass is soft and dry. Willows trail their leaves into a pool of water so clear you can see the white stones on the bottom. The air smells of honeysuckle. Pale lanterns of light drift among the branches, and somewhere close by, people are laughing and singing. Beyond the edge of the ring, the rot presses in on every side, grey and stinking, and the willows on that edge are blackened and dying.

## DM Notes
The last foothold of **[[Queen Nellye]]** in the swamp: a fey grove planted to hold back the taint from [[F7 – The Wyrm's Ribcage|the dead dragon]]. It is losing. Each year the rot takes a few more yards.

**The lights** that drift over [[D7 – The Seeping Margin]] are the grove's lanterns. They're fey lights, not will-o'-wisps: harmless, and they lead the lost here. No one comes back because the fey keep them as guests, and time runs differently in the grove.

**Fey time.** Each night spent in the grove is **1d4 days** outside. Guests feel like they've been here an evening. Tell the party only when they leave.

## Brannagh, Keeper of the Grove
A [dryad](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#dryad_mm), Nellye's servant, bound to the great willow at the pool. She is gentle, sad and very tired. She's polite but wary of mortals carrying iron or fire.
- **Wants:** the taint gone. She knows the dragon's bones are the source and that the obsidian splinter in them keeps the wound open.
- **Offers:** a safe night's rest (a long rest here also cures swamp fever), and a guide light to anywhere in the swamp.
- **Quest:** _Idea (unconfirmed):_ she gives the party a living seed to plant in the heart-pool at F7. See [[F7 – The Wyrm's Ribcage#Cleansing the Bones]].
- **(Rod)** Brannagh senses [[Beha]] at once. She turns cold and frightened, asks the party to leave the rod outside the ring, and warns them: _"That thing tried to kill my queen."_ If they refuse, the lights go out and the guests fall silent. The party gets no help here.

## The Guests
Four travellers from the Grassland who followed the lights: two farmhands, a tinker and a Naywich girl. They're dancing, eating and happy. They think they arrived last night. In truth, it's been months.
- Leading them home is a good deed with a sting: their families have given them up for dead.
- _Idea (unconfirmed):_ one is a missing child from a Grassland quest the party already knows about.

## Encounter: The Rot at the Edge
Each night, the rot sends blights against the grove's dying edge: 2 [needle blights](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#needle%20blight_mm), 1 [vine blight](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#vine%20blight_mm) and 6 [twig blights](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#twig%20blight_mm), grown from the tainted trees. Brannagh and 3 [sprites](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#sprite_mm) fight them alone if the party doesn't help. Helping wins Brannagh's trust.

## Loot
Brannagh doesn't value gold. If the party defends the grove, she gives each character a willow leaf that never wilts: it's a charm. While carried, the bearer has advantage on saves against swamp fever. It lasts until the bearer leaves the swamp.
