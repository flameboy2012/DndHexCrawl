## DM Notes
The **bullywug** mudholes. About twenty bullywugs, plus their giant frogs. They are primitive, greedy and cowardly, but vicious in numbers. They're at war with the lizardfolk of [[G8 – The Scaleback Warren]], who raid them for food and heads. The bullywugs hate the lizardfolk and steal anything shiny.

Bullywugs think of themselves as lords of the swamp, and they demand tribute (food, shiny things) from anyone passing through "their kingdom". Flattery works.

## Leader
**King Grubbo the Magnificent**, a fat old [bullywug](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#bullywug_mm) with maximum hit points, wearing a rusted crown from Qahessra. He rides a [giant toad](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#giant%20toad_mm) and sits on a throne of driftwood.
- **Wants:** the lizardfolk gone, and more shiny things.
- **Deal:** He offers the party a "royal reward" (his pile of junk, see _Loot_) for the head of Gethrak, the lizardfolk chief. Both sides want the party to fight the other.

## Encounter: The Royal Guard
If the party refuses to pay tribute, or is caught near the domes: 6 [bullywugs](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#bullywug_mm) and 2 [giant frogs](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#giant%20frog_mm) attack from the reeds. They flee once half of them are down.

## Loot
Grubbo's hoard, piled behind his throne: mostly junk (shells, buttons, a rusted helmet), but also 3d10 gp, a silver mirror (25 gp), and Grubbo's crown: a bronze circlet set with a cobra of green glass (the glass is worthless; the circlet is 40 gp to a collector).
