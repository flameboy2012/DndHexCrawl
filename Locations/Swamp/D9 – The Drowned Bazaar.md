_Ruin / Hazard / Biome Border_

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Rows of stone arches rise out of the swamp here, ankle-deep in green water. Between them run narrow lanes, lined with what were once shops: stone counters, empty doorways, and wooden shutters swollen and black with rot. Bubbles rise lazily through the water, and every so often one bursts with a smell like rotten eggs.
>
> Somewhere in the maze of arches, something heavy shifts in the water, and the stink grows suddenly much worse.

## DM Notes
The great **market quarter** of Qahessra: a maze of covered lanes, workshops and caravan-yards, now drowned. (Inspired by Cairo's Khan el-Khalili bazaar.) To the west it borders the [[C9 – The Sunken Stones|Sunken Stones]] and the dunes of the Oasis. To the south, the sea.

## Hazards
- **Swamp gas.** Gas pockets lie under the silt. Each hour in the bazaar, one character stirs one up (**DC 13 Constitution save** or poisoned for 1 hour). A naked flame ignites the gas: 3d6 fire damage in a 10-ft radius (**DC 13 Dex save** for half).
- **Sinking mud.** Some lanes have silted into sucking mud. **DC 12 Survival** to spot it. A character who blunders in sinks (see [[Swamp Random Encounters#Swamp Travel]]).
- **Getting lost.** The lanes all look alike. Finding the way out takes **DC 13 Survival**; failure costs an hour and another gas check.

## Encounter: The Midden
An [otyugh](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#otyugh_mm) lives in the drowned caravan-yard at the heart of the bazaar, in a heap of filth and bones washed in by the tide. It's hungry and telepathic: it projects images of food at the party, then of the party _as_ food. It may bargain: it lets the party pass in exchange for something rotten and large.
- Its bite risks disease (as normal for the otyugh).

## Loot
- **The moneychanger's stall:** under the counter, a bronze strongbox, rusted shut (**DC 15 Athletics** or thieves' tools). Inside: 4d10 ancient ouroboros coins (25 gp each) and a set of brass merchant's weights shaped like coiled snakes (50 gp the set).
- **In the midden:** buried in the otyugh's heap, a corroded ring of keys and a gold signet ring with a cobra seal (75 gp). _Idea (unconfirmed):_ the signet fits a lock in the vizier's tomb in [[E9 – The City of the Dead]].
