_Notable Site / Dragon Remains_

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The trees here are dead: grey, leafless, and dripping. The ground drops into a wide black hollow, and rising out of it is a row of great curved shapes, each as thick as a tree trunk and taller than a house. It takes you a moment to understand that they are ribs.
>
> A dragon's skeleton lies sprawled across the hollow, half sunk into the mud. Its skull, big enough to stable a horse in, rests on its side at the far end, jaws agape. Beneath the ribcage, where the heart would have been, a pool of thick black water steams gently. Nothing grows within fifty yards of it. Your eyes water, and the air tastes like copper.

## DM Notes
These are the bones of **Ysvarra the Black**, one of [[Factions#Other Key Players|Baram]]'s dragon flight. In the war she dived on [[Beha]]'s palace ([[F8 - Tomb of Beha]]), and Beha struck her down with his magic. She fell here, inside the walls of Qahessra. Her body rotted where it lay, and her acid blood soaked into the land. That rot is what turned Beha's city and its gardens into the swamp. (The "king's acid... no, theirs" tablet in [[C8 – The Dune Wastes]] records her fall.)

**The heart-pool** is the source of the taint. Black water seeps out from it in every direction. Anyone who drinks from it, or is submerged, must succeed on a **DC 15 Constitution save** or contract swamp fever (see [[Swamp Random Encounters#Swamp Travel]]).

**What the bones show.** **DC 13 Medicine or Nature:** the ribs on one side are shattered from the inside outwards. Lodged in the breastbone is a long splinter of black obsidian, the same glassy stone as the [[Rod of Beha]]. **(Rod)** The fragment stays quiet here unless the bearer asks about the bones. Then it says, with relish: _"She was the first. She screamed so beautifully."_

**Who cares about her.**
- **[[Jiaolong]]** knew her. It's why he avoids the swamp.
- **[[Queen Nellye]]** wants the taint cleansed (see [[D8 – The Lantern Grove]]).
- **The lizardfolk** of [[G8 – The Scaleback Warren]] worship the skull as "the Black Mother" and leave offerings at it: fish heads, shiny stones, bullywug skulls. They see trespassers here as raiders.

## Encounter: The Black Blood
A [black pudding](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#black%20pudding_mm) lives in the heart-pool, grown from the dragon's acid blood. It rises when anything disturbs the water or touches the obsidian splinter. Its acid dissolves wood and metal, so warn the party once they see a lizardfolk spear melt.
- If lizardfolk are present with an offering, they stop to watch. They take a pudding attack as the Black Mother's judgement.

## Loot
- **Dragon teeth:** 1d4 loose teeth in the skull, 50 gp each (or more to a wizard).
- **Black dragon scales:** about a dozen still sound. A skilled armourer could use them to make armour that resists acid. _Idea (unconfirmed):_ use the _armor of resistance (acid)_ rules if you want a reward here, at a cost in gold and weeks of work.
- **The obsidian splinter:** 2 ft long, cold, faintly magical (evocation). Worthless to sell, but [[D8 – The Lantern Grove|Brannagh]] says it must come out before the land can heal. _Idea (unconfirmed):_ the rod and the splinter come from the same stone, and the fragment wants it back.

## Cleansing the Bones
_Idea (unconfirmed):_ Brannagh in [[D8 – The Lantern Grove]] gives the party a living seed from Nellye's grove. Pull out the splinter, kill the pudding, and plant the seed in the heart-pool to start the healing. Over months the water clears from F7 outwards. This wins Queen Nellye's favour, and enrages the lizardfolk if they see it.
