_Ruin / Major Location_ A collapsed fortification of unknown age, its curtain wall breached and its tower a jagged stump. The road terminates here at a rusted portcullis. Something lives in the lower vaults—locals don't go near it after dark. The river can be heard rushing just beyond the eastern rubble.

**Tags:** Road terminus, ruin, dungeon entrance, river adjacent

## Additional Secret: The Dragon's Scar

Any character who spends time studying the melted stone throughout the keep can discover that the destruction wasn't caused by ordinary fire.

**DC 13 Arcana or Nature**

> The stone has partially vitrified into glass. Temperatures high enough to do this could only have come from an exceptionally powerful dragon.

**DC 15 Investigation**

> Several of the melted sections point inward rather than outward, suggesting the dragon may have landed within the courtyard before unleashing its breath.

This gives the party a mystery to ponder while exploring and sets up future adventures involving the dragon that destroyed Blackfire Keep.
