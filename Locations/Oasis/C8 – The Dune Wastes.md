_Wilderness Hex / Biome Border_

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Great dunes, some taller than a castle wall, roll away east toward a distant green smudge of swamp. The wind never lets up. It hisses across the crests and fills your footprints behind you almost as fast as you make them.
>
> Ahead, something square and grey juts from the side of a dune: the top of a stone tower, buried right up to its battlements. Each merlon is carved as a rearing cobra, hood spread. Not far from it, a dark shape lies half-covered in sand. A body.

## DM Notes
The wind reshapes the dunes overnight, burying some things and uncovering others. It exposed the **watchtower** only recently. Inside, sand fills the tower to within 10 ft of the roof.

## Encounter: The Hunter Below
A [bulette](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#bulette_mm) hunts the dunes, drawn by vibration. If the party digs at the tower or camps in the hex, it surfaces beneath them. A **DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)** check notices the dunes shivering a round before it breaks through.

## The Dead Scout
Near the tower lies a sand-scoured body in a pirate's sash: one of [[Captain Szolsassih]]'s scouts, killed by the bulette days ago. On the body:
- A cutlass, 14 gp, and a waterskin (empty).
- A hand-drawn map marking the [[A9 – The Pyramid of Beha|pyramid]] with the words _"the staff — find it before the others."_ Proof the pirates are searching, and that they still think it's a staff.
- A small bone token carved with an anchor wrapped in two serpents (the pirates' symbol), a mark of rank among the crew. Wearing it gets the party a hearing at the [[B9 – Pirate Camp|pirate camp]] instead of an attack.

## The Watchtower
Digging out the top room takes 2 hours (and draws the bulette). Inside: a bronze signal mirror, tarnished green (50 gp), and a cracked clay tablet in old yuan-ti script. **DC 15 History or comprehend languages:** a watch report — _"Wings over the eastern garden. The king's acid... no, theirs. The water turns to rot."_

The tablet records the fall of Ysvarra the Black, one of Baram's dragons, whose body rotted the city into the swamp (see [[F7 – The Wyrm's Ribcage]]).

## The Serpent Road
The eastern edge of the dunes hides the start of Beha's old processional causeway, which runs east into the swamp. The wind uncovers a stretch of its paving every few days. See [[Swamp Random Encounters#The Serpent Road]].
