_Wilderness Hex / Biome Transition_

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The last of the grass gives out beneath your boots, and the land becomes open sand. A haze of fine dust hangs in the air, turning the sun into a pale disc and swallowing every sound. Somewhere ahead, the pyramid looms in and out of view like a ship in fog.
>
> Out of the haze rises a dune with a face. No — a statue, colossal, toppled on its side and half-buried. A robed figure with the body of a serpent. Where its face should be, the stone has been torn away by four deep parallel gouges, each as wide as your arm. Its hands still clutch something carved across its chest.

## DM Notes
The haze is permanent here; the Haddari call it the "sandveil." The statue is [[Beha]]. The object in its hands is a carved rod, not a staff. Sand drifts have uncovered the base: a serpent devouring its own tail, cut deep enough to stand in.

## Hazard: The Veil
- Navigating through the hex requires a **DC 13 Wisdom (Survival)** check. On a failure the party wanders in a circle and arrives back at the statue after an hour, gaining a level of exhaustion if the weather is Hot and Dry or worse (see [[Weather]]).
- Ranged attacks beyond 60 ft have disadvantage while in the veil.

## Secrets
- **DC 14 Arcana or Nature:** the gouges in the face are claw marks from something enormous — a dragon. Ties to Baram's dragon flight (see [[Beha]]).
- **DC 15 Investigation:** the carved rod in the statue's hands is the same length and shape as the [[Rod of Beha]], not a staff. A clue for anyone who believes the "staff" rumour.
- **Rod reaction:** if the party carries the rod, the fragment speaks unprompted for the first time in a while: _"They took my face. They could not take my name."_ It will not explain further.
