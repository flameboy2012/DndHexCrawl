_Major Location / Dungeon_

![[pyramid keyed.jpg]]

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The pyramid fills the sky. Up close, its sandstone blocks are each the size of a cart, worn smooth by two thousand years of wind. Carved serpents coil up the corners toward the peak.
>
> Wrapped around the base is the biggest of them all: a colossal stone serpent, its body coiling around the pyramid until its head rests on the sand before you. Its jaws gape open, wide enough for a cart to pass through, and its fangs hang above you like portcullis spikes. Past its stone tongue, a dark passage runs down into the pyramid's throat. A cool, stale breath drifts out, smelling of dust and old spices.

## The Serpent's Mouth
The only way in is through the stone serpent's open jaws, which lead straight into room 1.
- _Idea (unconfirmed):_ the serpent's eyes are inlaid with dull red glass. If the party carries the [[Rod of Beha]] out through the mouth, the eyes light up and the jaws grind a few inches closer together, as if the pyramid were reluctant to let it go.

## DM Notes
Built by [[Beha]]'s court to hold the [[Rod of Beha]]. The rod lies in the chest in **room 9**, guarded by 2 bone nagas: priests bound to serve long after death.

**Source:** Act 2 of _The Pyramid Raid_ (MikWewa Tales, free, APL 7), condensed below in my own words so you can run it from this note. The map above is keyed with the adventure's room numbers (the unkeyed original is `pyramid.jpg`). The riddles and full room text are in the PDF.

[[Captain Szolsassih]]'s crew have tried the pyramid and lost several people. Scatter one or two dead pirates (anchor-and-serpents tattoos) in rooms 2, 3 or 6.

### Running Notes
- **The cats.** The 6 cat statues in room 1 choose a side. Pay each cat a gold coin and it fights for the party; refuse and it fights against them. Cats use [panther](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#panther_mm) stats. They leave room 1 when the party comes back through the room 4 door, and they turn up in fights, especially the room 9 fight. Use them to tune difficulty.
- **Mummy rot.** Mummy hits can curse characters. The cure is hidden on the obelisk in room 5; the bandages from the mummies in room 2 or 6 are part of it.
- **Bless of the Undergod.** Earned in room 10 using the bust from room 4. The next time a blessed creature drops to 0 hp, it stays on 1 hp instead. Until then, it is also immune to mummy rot. It lasts 24 hours or until it is used up. A creature can be blessed only once in its life.
- _Idea (unconfirmed):_ reflavour the "Undergod" as one of Beha's court gods, and make the broken statue in room 10 Beha himself, faceless like the statue in [[A8 – The Sandveil Approach]].

## Rooms

### 1. Entrance & Cat Statues
> [!quote] Read Aloud
> You pass down the serpent's throat into a long, torchlit hall. Six stone cats sit in a row, tails curled neatly round their paws, all staring back the way you came. To your left, a pair of doors stand shut with no handle and no lock.

- **DC 17 Investigation:** each cat holds out a paw with a coin-shaped dent in it. See _The cats_ above.
- The left-hand doors are barred from the other side; they open from room 4.

### 2. The Sleeping Mummies
> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A little further north, torchlight flickers in a small alcove. Two open stone sarcophagi rest there, and in each lies a wrapped and withered body.

- **Encounter:** 2 [mummies](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#mummy_mm). They lie still while the party loots and pokes them. When the party leaves the alcove, the mummies rise and attack from behind with +1 initiative. Burning the bodies first stops it.

### 3. Dart Pillars
> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A wide hall opens up, its roof held up by rows of thick pillars. Nothing moves. The floor is tiled in large square flagstones, and the silence is heavy.

- **Trap:** pressure plates across the floor fire darts from the pillars at anything Small or larger. **DC 15 Investigation** spots plates, but only within 5 ft of the searcher. **DC 15 thieves' tools** disarms one.
- Each dart deals 1d6 damage; roll 1d4 for the type: 1 necrotic, 2 poison, 3 acid, 4 piercing. Victims don't feel the damage until they leave the room.

### 4. Southwest Rooms
Reached by the first door on the left in room 3.
- **Store room:** crates of dry food and basic adventuring supplies. Give what the party needs.
- **South room:** a broken bust lies on the floor. It is the missing head from the statue in room 10.
- **East room:** 10 shiny spiders scatter from the party and freeze when bright light falls on them. They have AC 1 and 1 hp, and never attack. Killed, they crumble to dust. Left alone, they turn into gems worth **100 gp each**.
- **Far east door:** lift the bar to open into room 1. The cat statues are now gone.

### 5. The Obelisk
> [!quote] Read Aloud
> In an oval chamber stands a smooth black obelisk, plain and silent. Nothing about it seems to do anything.

- No check reveals anything. It carries an **invisible inscription** in an ancient language. Revealing it takes magic (for example _detect magic_ or _see invisibility_), and reading it needs _comprehend languages_ or the right language.
- **The inscription says:** to cure mummy rot, wrap a used mummy bandage round the victim's throat, then cast any healing spell on them or give them a healing potion.

### 6. Guards' Resting Place
> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A broad, high-ceilinged chamber. The furniture that once stood here has rotted to grey dust. At the far end gleams a golden sarcophagus, and four plainer stone coffins line the walls.

- **Encounter:** opening the golden sarcophagus wakes 4 [mummies](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#mummy_mm) from the side coffins. They were dwarf guards of the pyramid and wear chain mail (AC 16). Add cats.
- **Loot:** the golden sarcophagus holds an uncommon magic item of your choice.

### 7. The Vase Room
> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A door bars the way, carved with a single word in an old script: GREED. Beyond it you glimpse a small room packed floor to ceiling with clay vases.

- Only one character fits inside, and the door locks behind them. It opens again only when the puzzle is solved.
- Every vase holds a gold coin except one. **DC 15 Investigation** finds the empty one. **Solution:** put something of value into the empty vase.
- Taking a coin or breaking a vase before the puzzle is solved deals 1d4 force damage (a ghostly pain) per coin or vase.
- **Reward:** once solved, the door opens and the coins (10d10 gp total) can be taken freely.
- If a player is stuck, a hidden instruction on the wall gives a hint (Perception DC of your choice).

### 8. The Sphinxes
> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Two great stone sphinxes crouch on either side of a doorway in the north of the pyramid. As you approach, their eyes grind open.

- The sphinxes each ask a riddle. A wrong answer deals 1d6 force damage. A character killed by this turns to stone.
- **Answers:** _Memory_ and _Time_. Read the riddles from the PDF, or use these replacements:
  - _"I cannot be seen, yet I show you your mother's face. I am born in a moment and stay until you die. What am I?"_ (Memory)
  - _"Kings fear me, stone fears me, even the dragon grows grey under me. I eat mountains a grain at a time. What am I?"_ (Time)

### 9. Bone Naga's Rest — The Rod of Beha
> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The sphinxes fall still, and the way opens. The room beyond is barely lit. From the dark comes a sound like dry bones rattling together, and with it a slow, rising hiss.

- **Final encounter:** 2 [bone nagas](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#bone%20naga%20(guardian)_mm). They have advantage on initiative unless the party prepared before entering. All remaining cats join whichever side they chose.
- Cages along the south wall show the nagas were kept here as bound guardians, long after death.
- **Loot:** a chest carved with an eye rune holds the **[[Rod of Beha]]**. The fragment stays silent until someone attunes to it.
- Tuning: add cats to either side if the fight is too easy or too hard.

### 10. The Broken Statue
> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Two flights of stairs bring you to a northern chamber. Two stone guardians stand to either side of a central statue — a robed figure whose head has been broken off at the neck.

- Placing the bust from room 4 on the statue grants every character present the **Bless of the Undergod** (see Running Notes).

### 11. The Embalmer's Lab
> [!quote] Read Aloud
> This north-western room looks like a scholar's workshop. On the central table lies a mummy, its wrappings clean and fresh, and beside it an open book that is not nearly as dusty as it should be.

- **The book** is an embalming manual. A reader who passes a **DC 17 Medicine** check learns mummies' weak points: +1 to initiative and +1 to attack rolls against mummies.
- _Idea (unconfirmed):_ the fresh mummy is recent work. Someone, or something, is still embalming here.
