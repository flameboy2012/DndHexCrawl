_Ruin / Minor Site_

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The sand turns to grey, sucking mud, and the air smells of salt and rot. Here the desert, the swamp and the sea all meet. Out on the flats stands a ring of eleven tall stones, leaning at drunken angles and sunk to the waist in the muck. Gulls wheel over them, crying. Even from here you can see that the inner faces of the stones are carved.

## DM Notes
At high tide seawater floods the ring and only the tops of the stones show. At low tide a flight of steps appears in the centre, leading down to a flooded sunken court. Adjust the read-aloud text to the tide when the party arrives.

## The Mural
The inner faces of the stones are carved with a single continuous mural, worn but readable. Walking the ring clockwise tells a story:
1. A serpent king crowned with an ouroboros, worshipped by kneeling yuan-ti.
2. The king reading from a great book, holding a rod aloft.
3. The king and his army marching on a forest, toward a woman crowned in thorns and antlers ([[Queen Nellye]]).
4. The sky filled with dragons.
5–11. These stones have been deliberately smashed. Only fragments of wings, fire and a falling crown survive.

A party that reads it gets the clearest picture yet of [[Beha]]'s ascension attempt. The rod and the book in the carving match the [[Rod of Beha]] and [[Tome of Beha]].

## Encounter: The Last Priest
A [bone naga](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#bone%20naga%20(guardian)_mm) lies coiled in the flooded court below the steps: the remains of one of Beha's priests, bound to guard the court forever. It hisses in old yuan-ti and attacks anyone who descends.
- If the party carries the [[Rod of Beha]], the naga does not attack. It bows, and the fragment orders the party (through the attuned bearer) to _"leave my servant be."_ If the party destroys it anyway, the fragment is cold toward them for several days.
- Its lair holds 2 [giant crabs](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#giant%20crab_mm) that scuttle in from the sea at high tide.

## Loot
At the bottom of the flooded court (DC 12 Athletics to dive), a sealed stone urn holds: 3 ancient gold coins stamped with the ouroboros (25 gp each, worth far more to a collector or to the pirates), a _potion of water breathing_, and a jade snake-ring (a _ring of swimming_).

## Hazard
The tide turns every 6 hours. Anyone still in the sunken court when it floods must swim out (DC 13 Athletics) or be trapped underwater.
