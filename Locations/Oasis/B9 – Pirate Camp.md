_Major Location / Pirate Camp_

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> The palms come first: a ring of tall, green trees around a spring of clear blue water, the first real water you've seen in days. Then the smell of woodsmoke, tar and roasting fish. Spread across the sand from the spring to the shore is a sprawling camp of tents, dozens upon dozens of them, patched sailcloth in every colour, guy-ropes crossing like a spider's web. Stacked crates, barrels and coils of rope sit everywhere. Men and women in bright sashes mend nets, sharpen blades and argue over dice.
>
> Out past the surf, a great three-masted ship rides at anchor, her black sails furled. Longboats are drawn up on the beach below her. At the centre of the camp, larger than all the rest, stands a crimson pavilion. Above it flies a black flag bearing a white anchor, with two serpents coiled around its shaft and facing each other at the top.

## DM Notes
[[Captain Szolsassih]]'s crew landed here a month ago and took the oasis spring by force, driving the Haddari nomads out to [[B8 – The Dry Well]]. The camp holds **50–100 tents** and roughly **200 pirates**, most of them yuan-ti purebloods and human crew born into the band. The ship, the _Coiled Crown_, is anchored in B10. It is not explorable; a skeleton watch stays aboard.

The crew came for the "staff" of [[Beha]] and believe it lies in [[A9 – The Pyramid of Beha]]. They are right that it was there. But the pyramid has already killed several of Szolsassih's best people, and she will not throw away any more of her crew. Instead, she hires outsiders.

## The Camp
- **The Picket Line.** Lookouts on the dunes spot anyone approaching. Hesk meets newcomers with 6 armed crew. Adventurers are welcome, since the captain is hiring. Anyone else gets turned away.
- **The Spring.** Ringed with palms and guarded day and night. The crew rations water to no one but themselves.
- **The Tent Lanes.** Cookfires, dice games, a tattooist and a sailmaker. A good place to pick up rumours (see below). Wearing the bone sigil token from [[C8 – The Dune Wastes]] gets a warmer welcome.
- **The Stores.** Crates of salt fish, rum, rope and weapons. Hesk will sell basic gear at double price.
- **The Captain's Pavilion.** A crimson tent at the centre of camp, rich with carpets, lamps and brass. It is guarded by 2 yuan-ti malison. Inside, a shrine displays a cracked stone tablet showing the ouroboros, the crew's only relic of their ancestral kingdom.

**The crew's symbol** is an anchor with two serpents coiled around it, like a caduceus without wings. It appears on their flag and sails, and crew wear it as tattoos and on carved tokens. The crew don't use Beha's ouroboros as their badge; it is a holy relic to them.

## The Captain's Offer
Szolsassih sees any adventurers who come to camp. She is gracious and direct:
- The pyramid holds a **staff** that belonged to her ancestors' king. She wants it brought to her.
- She has already lost several of her crew inside, and she won't send more.
- **Pay:** 1,000 gp on delivery of the staff. The party keeps any other treasure they find. She supplies water and a guide to the pyramid's entrance.
- **What she knows.** No one who went in has come out, so she can only tell the party what her crew saw from outside:
  - She has sent two parties in through the serpent's mouth: first five of her crew, then three more who went to find them.
  - Those waiting at the mouth heard shouting deep inside, and once a long, dry rattling, "like bones in a sack." Then nothing.
  - The second party tied a rope to the serpent's fang and carried the other end in. When it went slack, they hauled it back and found the end cleanly cut.
  - She believes the staff lies "at the heart of the tomb, past whatever guards it." That is a guess, and she admits it.
- She is honest that she doesn't know what killed them. She'll pay an extra 50 gp for each of her dead brought back for burial at sea.

_DM note:_ her missing crew died in the pyramid. Use them as bodies in rooms 2, 3 and 6 (see [[A9 – The Pyramid of Beha]]). The rattling was the bone nagas in room 9; the rope was cut by the room 2 mummies when they rose behind the second party.

**If the party has already looted the pyramid:** Szolsassih will have heard the party went in and came out. She invites them to her pavilion to ask, very politely, what they found. She does not know the artifact is a rod. A convincing lie or a fake staff may satisfy her for a while. If she learns the truth, she wants the rod back and offers far more than 1,000 gp before she tries force.

**Rod reaction:** the fragment in the [[Rod of Beha]] recognises the bloodline. Near the pavilion it whispers to the bearer: _"My children. Look how far they have fallen."_
