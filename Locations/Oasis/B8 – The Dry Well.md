_Nomad Camp / Social Hub_

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> A ring of patched hide tents and kneeling camels huddles around an old stone well. Somebody is hauling on the well rope, hand over hand; the bucket comes up a quarter full of silty brown water, and a dozen people watch it like it's gold. Children peer at you from behind the tent flaps. A few of the adults have picked up their spears, though none of them have pointed them at you yet.

## DM Notes
The well is nearly dry. These are the **Haddari**, desert nomads who have walked the oasis lands for generations. Until a month ago they camped at the true oasis spring to the south. Then the pirates landed, took the spring for [[B9 – Pirate Camp|their camp]], and drove the Haddari off at blade point. Now they are slowly dying of thirst at a well that was never meant to support them.

## Lore the Haddari Know
- Their stories say the land was once "the Burning King's garden" until **the Dragon Lord** came down from the sky and broke it. The Haddari honour the Dragon Lord (Baram) and keep his icon. They do not know the king's name — only that saying it aloud is bad luck.
- They know the pyramid as "the King's Tomb" and never go near it. (They are wrong — his body lies in [[F8 - Tomb of Beha]].)
- They trade with **Ksarra**, a young brass dragon who visits every few weeks to hear news and gossip (see [[Oasis Random Encounters]]).

## Hooks
- **Retake the spring.** Hadra asks the party to get the pirates to share or leave the spring. She prefers a deal; Teo will raid alone if the party stalls, and probably die.
- **Water for passage.** The Haddari can guide the party safely through the [[A8 – The Sandveil Approach|sandveil]] and the [[C8 – The Dune Wastes|dune wastes]] (advantage on navigation checks) if the party brings them water or helps with the spring.
- **The dragon icon.** If the party carries the [[Rod of Beha]] openly, Hadra's icon grows warm and she becomes afraid of them. The fragment calls her people _"Baram's dogs."_
