_Yuan-ti Pirate Captain_

Location: [[B9 – Pirate Camp]]

Szolsassih and his crew are descendants of [[Beha]]'s yuan-ti followers who fled when Baram's dragons destroyed the kingdom. Their history has survived only in fragments.

## Wants
The [[Rod of Beha]] — an artifact linked to their ancestral past. Like nearly everyone, they believe it is a staff.

## Knows
Only fragments and rumours of what happened to Beha.

## Hook
The fragment in the rod may recognise Beha's bloodline in the pirates. It might try to rally them, or the crew might treat the rod as a holy relic.
