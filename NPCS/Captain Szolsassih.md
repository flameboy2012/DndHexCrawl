_Yuan-ti Pirate Captain_

Location: [[B9 – Pirate Camp]]

The pirates landed in the region only a month ago, on the opposite side of the map from [[I2 - Castle Dice|Castle Dice]]. [[Lady Dice]] is unaware of them.

Szolsassih and her crew are descendants of [[Beha]]'s yuan-ti followers who fled when Baram's dragons destroyed the kingdom. Their history has survived only in fragments.

She commands roughly 200 pirates from a large pavilion at the centre of camp. Her ship, the _Coiled Crown_, lies at anchor offshore in B10.

The crew's symbol is an anchor with two serpents coiled around it, like a caduceus without wings.

## Wants
The [[Rod of Beha]] — an artifact linked to their ancestral past. Like nearly everyone, they believe it is a staff.

The [[A9 – The Pyramid of Beha|pyramid]] has already killed several of her crew, and she refuses to risk more. She hires any adventurers who come to camp to go in for her: 1,000 gp for the staff, and the party keeps everything else.

## Knows
Only fragments and rumours of what happened to Beha.

## Hook
The fragment in the rod may recognise Beha's bloodline in the pirates. It might try to rally them, or the crew might treat the rod as a holy relic.
