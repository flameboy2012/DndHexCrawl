## What We Know So Far
- An ancient river dragon of eastern legend: serpentine and vast, with iridescent blue-green scales. Proud, formal, deeply territorial, and not immediately hostile. Expects tribute.
- The river that runs south through the Grassland rises from a sacred spring in his lair. He treats the river and its banks as his domain.
- One of Baram's dragons, and Baram's closest presence in the region (see [[Factions#Other Key Players]]).
- Tolerates Mother Dendra ([[E4 – Mother Dendra's Hovel]]). Thornshadow in [[A2 – Thornshade Camp]] keeps his existence secret.
- His river feeds the swamp, but he avoids it. The rot comes from the corpse of Ysvarra the Black, a fellow dragon of Baram's flight ([[F7 – The Wyrm's Ribcage]]). His scale stones in [[E7 – The Rotting Quay]] mark where his water ends.
- [[Lady Dice]] sees him as an obstacle: he blocks travel across the river.
