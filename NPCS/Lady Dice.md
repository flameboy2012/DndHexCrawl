## Goal
Lady Dice wants [[Beha]]'s knowledge and power to expand her frozen domain.

## Status
- She holds the [[Tome of Beha]].
- She sent her apprentice to raise Beha as undead in the [[F8 - Tomb of Beha|Tomb of Beha]], but he could not break the seals. The party interrupted the ritual and the apprentice fled by dimension door.
- That was the party's first encounter with her faction. She is now watching them.
- She does not yet know the [[Rod of Beha]] is the key to the seals. She will work it out eventually.

## Pressure (no fixed clock)
If the party stalls, Lady Dice pieces it together and starts hunting them. Use this to hurry them along.

Draft escalation steps:
1. Scouts or spies trail the party.
2. An agent approaches posing as an ally or buyer.
3. Her returned apprentice ambushes them.
4. Lady Dice comes in person.

## Endgame
The final battle is with Lady Dice over the tome. How it ends depends on who the party sides with.
