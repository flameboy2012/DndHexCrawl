_Lich / Divination Wizard / Mistress of [[I2 - Castle Dice]]_

## Who She Was
Around 200 years ago, Lady Dice was a human divination wizard. Her true name is forgotten.

To protect her lover, she attempted a ritual to **bind fate**. The ritual succeeded and her lover was saved, but it went wrong for her: her soul was split, part of it was sealed into a die, and she was turned into a lich. Her lover rejected her for what she had become and left her.

She was part reckless, part unlucky. She knows it was hubris, which is why she took the name "Lady Dice". She doesn't want pity; she focuses on her goals.

## Soul and Body
- **Body.** She still wears her original body, but lichification has twisted it into something skeletal.
- **Phylactery.** The die holding part of her soul. It sits in the Soul Vault of [[I2 - Castle Dice|Castle Dice]].
- **The lost half.** The rest of her soul was lost. Its fragments linger in the cracked mirror of the Sanctum of Reflection, which shows her as she once looked and speaks to whoever stands before it.
- She can't pass on while part of her soul is in the phylactery, and she doesn't want to destroy it.

## Goal
Lady Dice wants to **undo her lichification and return to her mortal self**, before she loses what is left of her humanity.

She has heard rumours of [[Beha]]'s power as a wizard and believes he can reverse lichdom.
- The [[Tome of Beha]] contains the _wish_ spell, but she knows a wish is a monkey's paw. She needs a way to make it reverse her condition without backfiring.
- She tried to raise Beha as undead to question him about whether the cure is possible and, if it is, make him perform it.
- She does **not** know about the fragment of Beha in the [[Rod of Beha]].

### The Trap
If she gets both the rod and the tome, she will attempt to cure herself, and Beha will possess her instead. She is the perfect vessel: a powerful wizard with no soul in her body. Beha then pursues his own agenda.

## Losing Her Humanity
She has roughly a quarter of her humanity left. As it fades she becomes colder, crueller, more driven, and hungry for power. The lich side already built most of Castle Dice: it began with the Soul Vault to protect her phylactery and grew as her humanity slipped. The frozen tundra is also a product of her curse, and would slowly return to normal if she were cured.

She misses the company of other people.

**Three strikes.** Each major stressful event is a strike (for example: a siege on Castle Dice, losing the rod to a rival, being humiliated by Drongo). On the third strike her humanity is gone. The party will most likely be the reason she tips over.

**When her humanity is gone,** she abandons her goal of returning to normal, accepts her fate, and takes her anger out on the surrounding lands. If the final battle with her begins while any humanity remains, that is the tipping point.

## Weakness
**Desperation.**

## Her Faction
- **Callus — Apprentice.** A necromancer who sought out Lady Dice to further his study of dark magic. He recovered the tome from Beha's grave in [[F8 - Tomb of Beha]], but failed to break the seals on the corpse. The party interrupted his ritual and he fled by dimension door.
- **Geoffrey — Majordomo.** The only servant who knows she wants to be human again. He supports her.
- **Commander Valerius — Army commander.** Commands the undead in the Crypt of Endless Service.

None of the other servants know her true goal. If they find out, infighting breaks out over who takes control.

She has no living agents in the southern regions.

## Relations
- **Queen Nellye** — Indifferent. Respects the power of the fey, but doesn't trust them.
- **Lady Hallowstride** — Aware of her, and careful not to be seen encroaching. Hallowstride mocks Dice's quest to be human again as foolish.
- **[[Captain Szolsassih]]** — Unaware of the pirates.
- **Drongo** — An annoying wizard trying to thwart her because she is an "evil lich". Currently a massive pain to her.
- **Baram** — An old god past his time, better forgotten.
- **Jiaolong** — Another pain: he blocks travel across the river. The sooner he's removed, the better.

## Lady Dice and the Party
- The party's interruption of Callus's ritual was their first encounter with her faction. For now she is only **watching** them.
- Once she learns they have the rod, she **invites them to Castle Dice to talk**.
- She will bargain for the rod. If asked, she is honest about why she wants it, as long as she thinks honesty helps her get it.
- The party can learn her true goal: a moral twist on a usually evil character.
- If the party stalls, she pieces it together sooner. Use this to hurry them along.

## Endgame
The final battle is with Lady Dice over the tome. All endings are possible — destroyed, cured, redeemed, or allied with the party — depending on the players' actions. For now, Beha is the only route to a cure, which keeps the adventure focused on him.
