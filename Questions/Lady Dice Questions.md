Answer under each question. Skip any you don't know yet; blanks show what still needs deciding.

## Current Context

- Lady Dice became a lich about 200 years ago through an **unlucky ritual that went wrong**. Her soul was split and placed in a die.
- She does **not** want to be a lich. She seeks [[Beha]]'s power to **undo the lichification and return to her body**.
- She knows Beha only from rumours of his power as a wizard.
- She is racing time: she must succeed **before she loses her humanity**.

Suggested priority: answer 6, 9, 13, 18, 22 and 29 first. They drive most of the rest.
